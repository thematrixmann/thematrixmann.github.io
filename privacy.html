MoneyMate – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · App: MoneyMate (Android, package com.dee.finance_tracker)

In short: MoneyMate keeps all your data on your phone. It has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission, so it cannot upload, share or sell anything about you.

1. Who we are

MoneyMate is a personal finance tracker made by an independent developer. Contact: thematrixmann@gmail.com.

2. Data the app stores

Everything you enter is saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone: accounts and balances, transactions, categories, loans and EMIs, planned payments, credit cards and statements, savings goals, people you lend to or borrow from, and your settings. This data never leaves your phone unless you export it (see section 5).

We (the developer) do not receive any of this data. We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts or usage statistics.

3. SMS messages (optional)

If you turn on From SMS, MoneyMate asks for Android's SMS permission (READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS) after showing you an explanation. It uses it only to:

  • read bank, UPI and credit-card messages on your phone to find transactions (amount, debit/credit, last digits of the account or card, merchant) and bill due dates, so you can add them with one tap;
  • show a "tap to add" notification when a new bank message arrives.

Messages are processed on your phone only. Messages that do not look like a bank or bill alert (personal messages, OTPs, offers) are ignored and not saved. Found transactions are only added to your records after you confirm them. SMS content is never uploaded, shared or sold. You can turn this off at any time in the app (From SMS › settings) or remove the permission in Android settings.

5. Backups and exports

Backup files, automatic backups and CSV exports are created only when you ask for them (or turn automatic backup on) and are saved to a folder on your phone that you choose or to Documents/MoneyMate Backups. What you do with those files (for example copying them to cloud storage) is up to you. Android's own cloud backup of app data is switched off for MoneyMate.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not share, sell or transfer any data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

7. Deleting your data

Use More › Backup & Restore › Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side to delete.

8. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add a PIN / fingerprint lock. Keep your backup files safe: anyone with a backup file can read its contents.

9. Children

MoneyMate is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
